Knowledge panel links
P973 (described at URL) on a Wikidata item tells search engines that the Ars Accordia page is an authoritative description of that artist. This is what surfaces the site in Google Knowledge Panels and Europeana aggregation.
P214 · P245
Identifier enrichment
Adding verified VIAF and Getty ULAN identifiers to Wikidata items strengthens the authority record for artists whose presence in the open knowledge graph is thin or incomplete — particularly Latvian and Baltic artists.
