Replaced the old weights-v2 (which included a corroboration multiplier) with the simpler weights-v3 model:

**`Passport Score = 100 × Completeness`** — no corroboration term, no source-quality judgement.

Key changes:
- `scripts/build_collection_score.py` — fully rewritten with `WEIGHTS_V3` constants. New band names: Complete Record / Substantial Record / Partial Record / Outline Record / Minimal Record. Authority fill now `min(1, confirmed_count / 2)` (counts independent public authorities, never grades them).
