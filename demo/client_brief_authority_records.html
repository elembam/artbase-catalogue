Why Authority Records Matter

For collectors, foundations, and institutional collections

A catalogue without authority records is documented for its owner. A catalogue with authority records is documented for everyone — auditors, insurers, customs, scholars, future stewards. The difference compounds over decades.

01 · Discoverability

Your collection joins the international record

When the artists in your collection are entered in Wikidata, Getty ULAN, and equivalent authorities, their works become findable in Google's Knowledge Graph, scholarly databases, AI-powered research tools, and global museum aggregators. A painting on a wall is private property; a painting linked to authoritative records is part of a discoverable cultural record.

02 · Value Protection

Identification beyond dispute

Authority links create unambiguous, third-party-verifiable identification. Insurance claims process more smoothly. Customs clearance is faster. Loan and exhibition negotiations open with documentation the receiving institution already trusts. If a work is ever stolen, lost, or contested, the chain of identification is robust because it does not rely on your own assertion alone.

03 · Scholarly Standing

Your collection contributes to art history

For established artists, your catalogue's links into international scholarship enrich the global record. For emerging or under-documented artists, your records may become the foundational documentation by which they are later studied — when an artist eventually warrants a catalogue raisonné, the editor's first request is to see the early professional records. Yours.

04 · Continuity

Documentation that outlives transactions

Authority records persist for decades, independent of any single institution. When a collection moves — through sale, donation, inheritance, or institutional transfer — properly linked records travel with it and remain intelligible to the next steward. Internal documentation that does not link to external authorities is harder to interpret a generation later, and harder to defend at audit.

What this work produces for your collection
Wikidata entries for every artist where notability is plausible
Getty ULAN nominations for artists meeting scholarly criteria
Authority cross-references on every artwork record
Persistent identifiers linking your catalogue to global systems

The work itself is documentation; the documentation is the work's durability.
