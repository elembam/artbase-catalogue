§ 04

Provenance

Documented Chain of Custody
c. 1503 — 1519
Leonardo da Vinci
Painted in Florence and worked on intermittently in Amboise. Possibly commissioned by Francesco del Giocondo; commission likely never delivered.
Source: Vasari, Le Vite, 1550 ed.; Zöllner, Leonardo da Vinci. Complete Paintings, 2019.
1519
Salaì (Gian Giacomo Caprotti)
Inherited from Leonardo at his death in Amboise.
Source: Leonardo's testament, Amboise, 23 April 1519.
c. 1518 — 1530
François I, King of France
Acquired for the French royal collection at Fontainebleau. Exact date and circumstances of acquisition disputed.
Source: Royal inventories, Fontainebleau.
1530 — 1797
French Royal Collection
Held at Fontainebleau, Versailles, and other royal residences. Briefly hung in Napoleon's bedroom at the Tuileries (1800 — 1804).
Source: Successive royal inventories.
1797 —
Musée du Louvre, Paris
Transferred to the Louvre following the French Revolution. Stolen by Vincenzo Peruggia 1911; recovered 1914. On continuous public display in Room 711 since.
Source: Louvre acquisition records; press coverage 1911 — 1914.
