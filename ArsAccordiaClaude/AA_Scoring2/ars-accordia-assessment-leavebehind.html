How well documented is your collection?

Now there is a number — and a plan to raise it.

The Ars Accordia Score measures how well documented your collection is — an open figure that grows as more of it is catalogued to standard, read alongside the standard of those records and the number documented. We don't reduce it to a percentage of a total that can rarely be known: an honest, open measure is worth more than a precise-looking guess.
