# Ars Accordia product backlog tied to Deloitte Art & Finance themes

## Goal
Translate Deloitte’s key themes into a practical product roadmap for Ars Accordia. The backlog is organized around the strongest commercial and strategic opportunities emerging from art market financialization, intergenerational wealth transfer, and trust-demand in wealth management.

## Product framing
The core product thesis is:
- Art collections require structured documentation to survive transfers, taxes, estate planning, valuation, and stewardship.
- The future market demands not only cataloguing, but record integrity, confidence, and genealogy of ownership.
- Ars Accordia should build the trust layer that supports that need.

## Priority themes from Deloitte
- intergenerational wealth transfer
- art as wealth and collection stewardship asset
- next-gen collector expectations
- trust, transparency, and professionalism
- art-secured lending and advisory use-cases
- estate planning and governance
- data, AI, and digitalization
- philanthropy and legacy

## Backlog structure

### Epic 1: Collection stewardship and transfer-readiness
Priority: P0

#### Features
1. Collection overview dashboard
   - summary of collection size, works, institution, provenance status
   - owner-facing view for a family or collection steward

2. Collection audit module
   - identify missing records, incomplete provenance, uncertain attribution
   - score collection completeness and risk

3. Inheritance readiness report
   - list works with missing provenance, condition concerns, or unclear ownership status
   - recommended next steps for heirs and executors

4. Collection transfer brief
   - exportable summary for family offices, estates, or advisors
   - includes key historical, documentary, and stewardship information

5. Stewardship action list
   - tasks to secure, document, condition-check, or digitize works

#### User stories
- As an heir, I want to understand what was inherited and how well it is documented.
- As a family office, I want a collection-level readiness summary before a transfer occurs.
- As an executor, I want a risk and documentation view of the estate.

#### Deloitte theme link
- next-gen wealth transfer
- collection governance
- family office needs
- stewardship and legacy

### Epic 2: Provenance inside the broader record
Priority: P0

#### Features
1. Provenance confidence scoring within the full object record
   - track provenance confidence by object and chain step
   - visually separate verified, uncertain, and missing provenance data without confusing it with the whole record

2. Authority and reference linking for the full record
   - links to institutions, archives, catalogues, auction records, and public mentions
   - show where provenance is supported or unsupported within the wider evidence framework

3. Record completeness scoring
   - assess how much evidence exists per work across identity, provenance, authority, and object data
   - support advisory and due diligence use-cases

4. Historical chain-of-ownership timeline
   - exhibit chronology of ownership, transfers, and major events as one layer of record history

5. Provenance notes and caveats within the object dossier
   - transparent classification of certainty and data quality without implying provenance is the whole product

#### User stories
- As a collector, I want to know which parts of the provenance history are verified versus uncertain.
- As an advisor, I want a record that is explainable and legible to clients.
- As an insurer, I want consistent provenance evidence and documentation quality.

#### Deloitte theme link
- trust and transparency
- regulation and risk management
- governance and professionalism
- provenance as a layer inside a larger record architecture

### Epic 3: Estate planning and family governance tools
Priority: P1

Note: these tools depend on the broader object record, not only provenance history. Family heirs need more than a chain of ownership; they need the full picture of the work, its identity, references, and evidence.

#### Features
1. Family collection governance map
   - identify key people, roles, and responsibilities for ownership decisions

2. Estate planning snapshot
   - summary of works, values, records, and conditions for legal or advisor handoff

3. Decision support for heirs
   - compare sentiment value, provenance value, market relevance, and stewardship burden

4. Family communication page
   - explain collection history and key artworks in a readable, shareable form

5. Transfer-out scenario planner
   - estimate consequences of sale, donation, retention, or partition

#### User stories
- As a family member, I want to understand the consequences of keeping, selling, or donating an artwork.
- As an estate advisor, I want a readable collection summary for planning and communication.
- As a collector, I want a structured record to make legacy decisions without ad hoc knowledge loss.

#### Deloitte theme link
- wealth transfer
- family governance
- legacy and identity
- estate planning

### Epic 4: Art-secured lending and valuation support
Priority: P1

2. Valuation support pack

3. Risk and documentation flags

4. Lending eligibility indicators

5. Documentation request workflow

#### User stories
- As a lender, I want a clean evidence package to understand collateral quality.
- As a collector, I want to know whether an object is supported by records sufficient for financing conversations.
- As a valuation advisor, I want access to a structured record set rather than a fragmented archive.

### Epic 7: Data, AI, and digitalization infrastructure
Priority: P1

#### Features
1. Structured metadata schema
   - canonical fields for provenance, condition, ownership, and documentation

2. AI-assisted research assistant

3. Document ingestion and normalization

4. Search across provenance and object data

5. Confidence-aware intelligence layer

#### User stories
- As a researcher, I want AI help surfacing likely provenance references and missing data.
- As a steward, I want the system to distinguish verified records from uncertain information.
- As a product team, I want an interoperable, machine-readable record model.

## Summary
The Deloitte report is a clear signal: the future of art markets is not only about prices, galleries, and collectors; it is about documentation, stewardship, trust, and the ability to transfer complex collections across generations with confidence.

Ars Accordia should build the product around those realities:
- trustable records
- collection stewardship
- inheritance clarity
- provenance intelligence
- financial and institutional readiness
- next-generation usability

This is the correct strategic lane for the business: not merely cataloguing art, but enabling the art collection to remain legible, manageable, and valuable across generations.
