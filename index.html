What is Ars Accordia?

Ars Accordia issues Artwork Passports — standards-based identity documents for works of art held in European private and corporate collections. Each passport records identity, provenance, condition, and authority links at museum standards, compliant with ICOM Object ID, CDWA, and LIDO 1.1.

The catalogue serves as a public registry for art that does not appear in national museum catalogues or on Europeana — making it discoverable, verifiable, and permanently identified for scholarship, insurance, loans, and cultural heritage protection.

Want to document your collection properly?

Ars Accordia catalogues works of art held in private and corporate collections that are undocumented, unstandardised, or absent from scholarship — assigning each a permanent Artwork Passport at museum standards.

Corporate holdings, private collections, estate inventories, and institutional art — all catalogued against ICOM Object ID, Getty vocabularies, and Wikidata authority records.
